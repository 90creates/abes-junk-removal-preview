FAQ

Palm Tree Trimming questions, answered

Have a question we didn’t cover? Call or text (909) 708-0853.

When should palm trees be trimmed in California?

Most palms only need trimming about once a year. Many homeowners schedule it in late spring or summer to remove dead fronds and seed pods before they drop.

How much does palm tree trimming cost?

It depends on the height, the number of palms, and how much needs to come off. Estimates are free. Text a photo to (909) 708-0853.

Can you trim too much off a palm?

Yes. Cutting healthy green fronds weakens the palm. We remove dead and dying fronds and seed pods and leave the healthy canopy.

Do you remove palm trees?

Yes. Tree removal and stump grinding are part of our tree services.
